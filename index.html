About the Competition

Your Canvas. Our Universe. An Infinite Future.

The Astronomy & Space Study Center (ASSC) invites schoolchildren, university students, and eligible creative participants to participate in the Open Painting Competition 2026, under the theme ‘MAN, MOON & FUTURE’.

This competition provides a platform to express creativity, explore astronomy and space, and imagine humanity's future beyond Earth. Whether you paint with brushes or create with digital tools, your vision of the cosmos deserves to be seen.

Important: AI Artwork Policy

Please read this section carefully before submitting your artwork.

Categories 1 & 2

For Category 1 (Ages 6–12) and Category 2 (Ages 13–21), AI assistance and AI-generated content are strictly prohibited.

This includes using AI for:

  • Generating artwork
  • Modifying artwork
  • Enhancing artwork
  • Editing artwork
  • Altering artwork

Entries must be the participant's own original creative work.

Category 3 — Open Digital Art

AI-assisted artwork is allowed in the Open Digital Art category.

This category is open to participants of any age and accepts digital artwork created using digital software, including AI-assisted tools.
