Privacy & Data Use

How your registration information is handled.

This page provides a summary of data handling practices. The complete privacy policy is subject to organiser approval before public launch. Sections marked with “Awaiting Confirmation” require organiser review.

Information Collected

The registration form collects the following information:

  • • Participant name, date of birth, age, gender, and country of residence
  • • Competition category and artwork details
  • • School or institution details (optional)
  • • Contact email and phone number
  • • Parent/guardian information for participants under 18
  • • Competition-related acknowledgements and consent

How Information Is Stored

Registration responses are collected through Google Forms and stored in Google Sheets. Access to registration data is restricted to authorised competition organisers. Registration data is not stored in the website application itself or made available in client-side code.

How Information Is Used

Registration information is used for:

  • • Processing competition registration and artwork submissions
  • • Communicating submission confirmations and competition updates
  • • Verifying participant eligibility and category placement
  • • Contacting parents/guardians for participants under 18
  • • Award and certificate administration

Information About Minors

This competition includes participants as young as 6 years old. Parent or guardian consent is required for participants under 18. Personal information of minors is treated with special care and is only accessible to authorised organisers. Participant names, artwork, and identifying details are not published without the appropriate consent.

Publicity & Artwork Display

The registration form includes an optional consent checkbox for artwork and name display in competition publicity. This consent is entirely optional and is not required for registration. Artwork will not be displayed publicly without the appropriate consent.

Data Retention & DeletionAwaiting Confirmation

Details about data retention periods, deletion requests, and participant rights regarding their personal information are pending organiser approval and will be published before the competition launch.

Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services:

  • • Google Forms — registration form hosting
  • • Google Sheets — response storage
  • • Vercel — website hosting
  • • Google Fonts — web typography

No additional analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used.
