Competition Guidelines

Everything you need to know about participating in the Open Painting Competition 2026.

Theme & Organiser

Theme: MAN, MOON & FUTURE

Organiser: Astronomy & Space Study Center (ASSC)

Submission Deadline: 15 December 2026

Artwork Creation Standards

Authentic Expression • Astronomical Imagination

Whether painting on traditional canvas or digital tools, preserve original progress files and reference material.

Competition Categories

Category 1 — Children

No AI

Ages 6–12

An accessible category for young creative minds exploring the Universe through traditional artwork.

Category 2 — Youth

No AI

Ages 13–21

For young artists and students exploring astronomy, space exploration, and possible futures beyond Earth.

Category 3 — Open Digital Art

AI Allowed

Open to all ages

Accepts digital artwork created using digital software, including AI-assisted artwork.

AI Artwork Policy

Prohibited — Categories 1 & 2

AI assistance and AI-generated content are strictly prohibited.

  • Generating artwork using AI
  • Modifying artwork using AI
  • Enhancing artwork using AI
  • Editing artwork using AI
  • Altering artwork using AI

Entries must be the participant's own original creative work.

Allowed — Category 3

AI-assisted artwork is allowed in the Open Digital Art category. This category is open to participants of any age and accepts digital artwork created using digital software, including AI-assisted tools.

Submission Rules

  • Maximum of 2 artworks per participant.
  • Artwork is submitted directly via the submission section of the official Google Form.
  • Ensure your artwork file (JPEG, JPG, or PNG) is ready before completing the form.
  • Accepted digital submission file formats: JPEG, JPG, PNG.
  • Artwork must comply with originality requirements and category-specific rules.
  • Selected award ceremony participants must bring their original physical artwork, where applicable.
  • All submissions must meet the 15 December 2026 deadline.
Official Competition Protocols

Additional Guidelines

Detailed standards for artwork preparation, thematic adherence, evaluation criteria, and exhibition rights under the official theme “MAN, MOON & FUTURE”.

Artwork Specifications & Formatting

  • Digital Submissions: Scanned copies of physical paintings or original digital artworks must be uploaded in high-resolution JPEG, PNG, or PDF format. Ensure the file size does not exceed 25 MB to allow for smooth processing through the Google Form.
  • File Naming: Please rename your digital file using the format: Category_YourFullName (e.g., DigitalArt_KasunPerera.jpg) prior to uploading.

Originality & Theme Relevance

  • Authenticity: All artwork must be the original, unaided creation of the participant. Plagiarism, direct tracing, or the use of AI image generators (such as Midjourney or DALL-E) is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Theme Adherence: Artworks must clearly reflect the “MAN, MOON & FUTURE” concept. Submissions that stray significantly from this theme will not be scored.

Judging Criteria

  • Evaluation Focus: Submissions will be evaluated on thematic interpretation, creative composition, artistic technique, and overall visual impact by the judging panel selected by the Astronomy & Space Study Center (ASSC).
  • Finality: All decisions made by the judging panel and the organizing committee are final and binding.

Disqualification Policies

  • Submissions received after the designated deadline or submitted with corrupted/inaccessible files will not be evaluated.
  • Any artwork containing inappropriate, offensive, or explicitly political content will be rejected.

Rights & Award Logistics

  • Usage Rights: By submitting, participants grant the organizers the right to display the artworks in online galleries, promotional materials, or future exhibitions, always with full credit given to the original artist.
  • Winner Announcements: Shortlisted candidates and winners will be contacted directly via the email addresses provided during registration. Details regarding the certificate distribution or award ceremony will be communicated closer to the finalized date.
