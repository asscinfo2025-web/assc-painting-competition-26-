Registration & Artwork Submission

Complete the official form to register and submit your artwork for the Open Painting Competition 2026.

Age Categories

Children (6–12), Youth (13–21), and Open Digital Art (all ages). Choose the category that matches your eligibility.

AI Policy

AI assistance is prohibited in Categories 1 & 2. AI-assisted artwork is only allowed in Open Digital Art.

Direct Artwork Submission Included

The form includes a dedicated artwork submission section. You can upload your artwork files (up to 2 artworks in JPEG, JPG, PNG) directly while completing your registration.
